🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme highlights a major challenge in deploying AI in the enterprise: integrating with legacy systems.

1.  Legacy Systems: Many large organizations, especially in sectors like healthcare and finance, rely on old, mission-critical systems that are difficult and risky to replace.
2.  The Integration Gap: A state-of-the-art AI model is useless if it can't get the data it needs from these legacy systems, or if the underlying hardware is too slow to run it effectively.
3.  Service Level Agreements (SLAs): In an IT context, an SLA is a contract that defines the level of service expected from a provider. For an AI system, this could include guarantees about uptime, response time, and accuracy. Meeting these SLAs can be incredibly stressful when you're dependent on unreliable legacy infrastructure. This is why a significant part of any real-world AI project is focused on data engineering, infrastructure, and integration, not just the "glamorous" model-building part.
