🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme humorously highlights a key challenge in robotics and autonomous systems: energy management.

1.  Battery Life: For any mobile robot, from a delivery drone to a Mars rover, battery life is a critical constraint. All of its capabilities (flight, sensing, computation) depend on a limited power source.
2.  Autonomous Charging: A key area of research is developing autonomous charging capabilities. This could involve drones that can automatically fly back to a charging station when their battery is low, or even drones that can charge each other mid-air.
3.  Power-Aware Planning: More advanced autonomous systems incorporate energy constraints into their decision-making. For example, a drone might choose a shorter, more energy-efficient route if it knows its battery is low, rather than the most direct route.
