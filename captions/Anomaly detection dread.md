🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
Anomaly Detection (or outlier detection) is the process of identifying data points, events, or observations that deviate significantly from the majority of the data. It has many important applications:
• Fraud Detection: Identifying unusual credit card transactions.
• Cybersecurity: Detecting strange patterns in network traffic that could indicate an attack.
• Industrial Monitoring: Finding unusual sensor readings in a manufacturing plant that might signal a machine failure.
• Health Monitoring: Spotting abnormal vital signs in a patient.

Models can be built using various techniques, from simple statistical methods (e.g., anything outside of 3 standard deviations is an anomaly) to more complex machine learning models like Isolation Forests or autoencoders.
