🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
The "garbage in, garbage out" principle is the golden rule of machine learning. No matter how sophisticated your algorithm is, if your data is flawed, your model's predictions will be useless. Data cleaning (or data wrangling/preprocessing) involves several critical steps:

1.  Handling Missing Values: Deciding whether to remove rows with missing data, or to "impute" them by filling in a mean, median, or more advanced model-based prediction.
2.  Correcting Inconsistent Data: Fixing typos, standardizing units (e.g., converting everything to kilograms), and ensuring categorical data is uniform (e.g., "USA" vs "United States").
3.  Removing Outliers: Identifying and deciding how to handle data points that are drastically different from the rest, as they can skew the model's learning process.
4.  Feature Engineering: Creating new, more informative features from the existing data.

While tools like Pandas in Python make these tasks possible, they require careful thought and can be incredibly time-consuming, often taking up the majority of a project's timeline.
