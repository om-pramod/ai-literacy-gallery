🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme highlights the critical importance of AI safety and alignment.

1.  AI Safety: This field of research is focused on ensuring that AI systems operate as intended without causing unintended harm. In a system like an aircraft's autopilot, safety is paramount. This involves rigorous testing, verification, and validation to prevent catastrophic failures.

2.  AI Alignment: This is the challenge of ensuring that an AI's goals are aligned with human values. The "copilot" in the meme is an example of a misaligned AI. It might be technically proficient but its goals are not what the user (or society) desires. The alignment problem becomes more critical as AI systems become more autonomous and powerful.
