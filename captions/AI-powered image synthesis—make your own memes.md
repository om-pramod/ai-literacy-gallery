🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This is a perfect analogy for supervised learning.

1.  Training Data: In supervised learning, an AI model is trained on a dataset where the inputs are paired with the correct outputs. In this analogy, the "old exams" are the training data. The questions are the inputs, and the correct answers are the labels.
2.  Learning: The model (the student) studies this training data to learn the patterns and relationships between the questions and answers.
3.  Inference/Testing: The "final exam" is the test data. The model is given new, unseen inputs (new questions) and is expected to produce the correct outputs (answers) based on what it learned from the training data. If the final exam is too similar to the training data, the model might do well without truly "learning" the subject – a phenomenon related to overfitting.
