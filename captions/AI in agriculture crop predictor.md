🧐 For those who don't get it:
This meme humorously shows the result of "style transfer," an AI technique that can apply the artistic style of one image to the content of another. The result is often a surreal and funny blend of the two, as seen here with a selfie getting a "Picasso" makeover.

🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme showcases Neural Style Transfer. This technique uses a deep neural network, typically a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), to separate the "content" of an image from its "style."

1.  Content Representation: The model extracts the high-level features and objects from the content image (e.g., the person's face in the selfie).
2.  Style Representation: It captures the textures, colors, and patterns from the style image (e.g., a Picasso painting).
3.  Image Generation: The model then generates a new image that minimizes both the "content loss" (how different it is from the content image) and the "style loss" (how different it is from the style image). The result is an image that has the content of the first and the style of the second.
