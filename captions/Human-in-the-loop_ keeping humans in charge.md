🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a great illustration of the Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) concept.

1.  Human-in-the-Loop: This is a model of interaction where an AI system and a human work together to solve a problem. The AI does the heavy lifting, but it relies on a human to provide input in certain situations, especially when the AI is not confident in its own decision.
2.  CAPTCHA as HITL: CAPTCHA (Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart) is a classic example. It's a task designed to be easy for humans and hard for bots. Interestingly, the data from millions of users solving CAPTCHAs (e.g., labeling images) has been used as training data to improve computer vision models, creating a massive HITL data labeling system.
3.  The AI Irony: The irony is that modern computer vision models are now so good that they can often solve CAPTCHAs better than humans. The nature of CAPTCHA is constantly evolving to stay ahead of AI.
