🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
A/B Testing is a method of comparing two versions of something (like a webpage, app feature, or AI model) to see which one performs better.

1.  Hypothesis: You start with a hypothesis, e.g., "The new AI recommendation algorithm (Version B) will lead to more user clicks than the old one (Version A)."
2.  Splitting Traffic: You randomly split your users into two groups. Group A sees Version A, and Group B sees Version B.
3.  Measuring: You track a key metric (like click-through rate, conversion rate, or user engagement) for both groups over a period of time.
4.  Analysis: You use statistical analysis to determine if there is a significant difference in the metric between the two versions. If Version B performs significantly better, you have a winner and can roll it out to all users.

A/B testing is crucial for making data-driven decisions rather than relying on intuition or opinion.
