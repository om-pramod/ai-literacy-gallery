🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme touches on a fascinating area of AI ethics and capabilities: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) vs. Narrow AI. The AI we have today is "Narrow AI." It's incredibly powerful at specific tasks (like playing chess, translating languages, or even generating text), but it lacks true understanding, consciousness, or common-sense reasoning.

An AI chatbot might be trained on countless self-help books and biographies, enabling it to generate perfectly logical advice like "pursue work that aligns with your values." However, it's crucial to treat it as a very advanced (but not all-knowing) assistant, not a life guru.
