🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
Zero-Shot Learning (ZSL) is a fascinating capability of some modern AI models. It's the ability to correctly perform a task without having received any specific training examples for that task.

For example, a model trained on images of horses, tigers, and pandas might be able to correctly identify a "zebra" if it has also been trained on text that describes a zebra as a "striped horse-like animal." The model combines its visual knowledge of horses with its textual understanding of "striped" to make an inference about a new class it has never "seen" before. It's a key step towards more general and flexible AI.
