🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a commentary on AI washing and marketing hype.

1.  AI Washing: This is the practice of making exaggerated or misleading claims about how a product uses artificial intelligence. It's a way to capitalize on the public's excitement and interest in AI to make a product seem more advanced or valuable than it actually is.
2.  What "AI-enhanced" could mean:
• Meaningful AI: An "AI-enhanced" smart home hub could use machine learning to learn your routines and optimize your energy usage.
• Meaningless AI (The Candle): It's hard to imagine how a candle would be "AI-enhanced." Perhaps the company used an LLM to generate the marketing copy or the scent description. While technically a use of AI, it doesn't affect the core function of the product at all.
3.  The Parallel to "Cloud Washing": This is similar to the trend of "cloud washing" a few years ago, where companies would rebrand their existing products as "cloud-based" to seem more modern. It's a classic example of marketing trying to ride the wave of a new technology trend.
