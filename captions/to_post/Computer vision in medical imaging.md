🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme highlights a critical challenge in medical AI: robustness and edge cases.

1.  Computer Vision in Medicine: AI, particularly deep learning models like Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), has shown incredible promise in analyzing medical images (X-rays, CT scans, MRIs) to detect diseases like cancer. They can often spot patterns that are subtle or invisible to the human eye.
2.  The "Out-of-Distribution" Problem: AI models are only reliable when the data they see in the real world is similar to the data they were trained on. A burrito is an "out-of-distribution" sample – it's completely different from the medical images the model was trained on. The model has no concept of "burrito," so it tries to classify it as the closest thing it knows, which in this case might be a tumor.
3.  Importance of Human Oversight: This is why AI in medicine is almost always used as a tool to *assist* doctors, not replace them. A radiologist would immediately recognize the object as a burrito, providing a crucial layer of human common-sense reasoning and safety.
