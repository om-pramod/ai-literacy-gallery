🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme highlights a common misconception about Differential Privacy (DP).

1.  What DP Protects: Differential Privacy is not about encrypting data or preventing all leaks. It's a property of an algorithm or a query. It ensures that the *output* of an analysis does not reveal whether any particular individual was part of the input dataset.
2.  Implementation is Key: The privacy guarantees of DP depend entirely on its correct implementation. A flawed implementation could indeed leak more information than intended.
3.  The Epsilon Parameter (ε): The level of privacy in a differentially private system is controlled by a parameter called epsilon (ε). A smaller epsilon means more privacy (and more noise added to the data), while a larger epsilon means less privacy (and more accurate results). Choosing the right epsilon is a critical trade-off between privacy and utility. A poorly chosen epsilon could lead to the kind of "leak" joked about in the meme.
