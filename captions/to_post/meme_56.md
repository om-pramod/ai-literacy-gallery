🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a great summary of the current state of AI's impact.

1.  The AI's Burden (Large-Scale Optimization): AI is being used to solve incredibly complex optimization and prediction problems at a scale that is impossible for humans.
• Logistics: Optimizing shipping routes for companies like FedEx and Amazon.
• Finance: Algorithmic trading and fraud detection.
• Science: Protein folding (AlphaFold), drug discovery, and climate modeling.
    These are tasks where AI's ability to process vast amounts of data and find subtle patterns provides enormous value.
2.  The Human's Burden (Cognitive Tasks): While AI excels at large-scale data problems, humans are still essential for tasks that require creativity, critical thinking, emotional intelligence, and common sense. However, as the meme humorously points out, we also spend a lot of our brainpower on mundane tasks like spelling. This is where AI augmentation (like a spell checker) comes in, freeing up our cognitive resources for more important things.
