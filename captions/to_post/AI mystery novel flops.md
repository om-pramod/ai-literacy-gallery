🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a humorous take on a real challenge in AI-generated content: hallucinations and artifacts.

1.  NaN (Not a Number): In computing, NaN is a value that represents an undefined or unrepresentable result, often from a mathematical operation like dividing zero by zero. It's a sign that something has gone wrong in the calculations.
2.  Generative AI Artifacts: When a generative AI model (like an LLM) produces content, it can sometimes include strange artifacts or nonsensical outputs, especially if it encounters an edge case or an internal error. The model doesn't "know" that NaN is an error; it might just be a token that appears in its training data in certain technical contexts. The model then incorporates this token into the story, leading to a nonsensical result.
