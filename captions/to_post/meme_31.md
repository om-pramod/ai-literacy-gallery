🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a playful commentary on algorithmic bias and automation bias.

1.  Algorithmic Bias: If an AI system is designed to both create and judge art, its criteria for "good" art will be based on the patterns it learned during training. It might develop a preference for a certain style (perhaps its own) and unfairly penalize other styles. The judging process would be inherently biased towards the AI's own creations.
2.  Automation Bias: This is the tendency for humans to over-trust and favor decisions made by automated systems. If an AI is the judge, we might be tempted to accept its verdict without question, even if it's biased. This highlights the need for human oversight and transparency in any automated decision-making process, especially subjective ones like judging art.
