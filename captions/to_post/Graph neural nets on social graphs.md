🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) are a type of neural network designed to work directly with graph-structured data.

1.  Social Graphs: A social network is a perfect example of a graph. Each person is a node, and the connections (friendships, follows) between them are edges.
2.  How GNNs Work: GNNs learn by aggregating information from a node's neighbors. To learn a representation for you, the GNN would look at the features of your friends, and your friends' friends, and so on. This allows the model to capture the complex relationships and community structures within the network.
3.  Applications: GNNs are used in social networks for tasks like:
• Recommendation: Recommending new friends or content.
• Community Detection: Identifying groups of people with shared interests.
• Link Prediction: Predicting who you might become friends with in the future.
