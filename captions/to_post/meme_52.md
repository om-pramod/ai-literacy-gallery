🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme contrasts the two main "personas" of AI in the public imagination.

1.  Hollywood AI (AGI/Superintelligence): This is the AI of movies like *The Terminator* or *The Matrix*. It's sentient, has its own motivations (usually evil), and is often embodied in a humanoid robot. This is a representation of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), which does not yet exist.
2.  Real-World AI (Narrow AI/Machine Learning): The AI we use every day is Narrow AI. It's a set of statistical techniques and algorithms designed to perform specific tasks.
• Your Netflix recommendations are powered by machine learning.
• Your spam filter is a text classification model.
• Siri and Alexa use Natural Language Processing.
    These systems are incredibly powerful and useful, but they are not sentient or self-aware. They are complex mathematical models, not evil robots.
