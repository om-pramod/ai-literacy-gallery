🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
Robotic Process Automation (RPA) is a technology that uses software "bots" to automate repetitive, rule-based digital tasks that are typically performed by humans.

It's important to distinguish RPA from AI:
• RPA is about doing: RPA bots are "doers." They follow a set of pre-programmed instructions to interact with user interfaces, just like a human would. They click buttons, copy-paste data, fill out forms, and move files. They are great for automating legacy systems that don't have APIs.
• AI is about thinking: AI, on the other hand, is about simulating human intelligence. It involves learning, reasoning, and decision-making.

The two are often used together in what's called "Intelligent Automation," where AI provides the "brains" to make decisions, and RPA provides the "hands" to execute the tasks.
