🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a funny take on user expectations and the limitations of AI.

1.  Narrow AI: The AI we have today is "Narrow AI." It can be incredibly powerful at specific tasks (writing text, organizing lists), but it is not a general intelligence (AGI).
2.  The "Duck Conference" Problem: Planning a "duck conference" is a complex, multi-step task that requires:
• Common Sense Reasoning: Understanding what a "duck conference" is (which is nonsensical, and the AI would have no training data for it).
• Real-World Knowledge: Knowing about locations, travel logistics, etc.
• Planning and Execution: The ability to book venues, contact speakers (who are ducks?), and create an itinerary.
3.  AI Agents: While a single prompt to a standard LLM would fail, this is exactly the kind of problem that emerging AI agents are designed to tackle. An AI agent could (theoretically) break down the goal into sub-tasks, search the web for information, and interact with other tools to piece together a plan. However, it would likely still get stuck on the nonsensical premise of a "duck conference."
