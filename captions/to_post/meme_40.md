🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme highlights the importance of style and tone control in generative AI.

1.  Training Data Influence: An LLM's writing style is heavily influenced by its training data. Since a large portion of that data comes from the informal internet (social media, forums, etc.), the model is very good at generating casual language.
2.  Prompting for Tone: To get the right tone, you need to be explicit in your prompt. For example, instead of "write a reply to my boss," you should write "write a professional and formal email replying to my boss, thanking them for the opportunity..."
3.  Fine-Tuning: For enterprise applications, companies will often fine-tune a base LLM on their own data (e.g., their internal emails and documents). This helps the model learn the specific tone, style, and jargon of the company, ensuring that its outputs are always brand-safe and professional.
