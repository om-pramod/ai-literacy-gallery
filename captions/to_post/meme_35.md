🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is about the problem of AI hallucinations.

1.  What are Hallucinations?: In the context of LLMs, a hallucination is when the model generates text that is nonsensical, factually incorrect, or not grounded in its training data, but presents it as a fact.
2.  Why do they happen?: LLMs are not databases of facts; they are incredibly complex pattern-matching machines. They learn to predict the next most probable word in a sequence. Sometimes, the most probable-sounding sequence of words is not factually correct. The model doesn't "know" it's wrong; it's just following the statistical patterns it learned.
3.  The Confidence Problem: A key issue is that LLMs don't have a built-in sense of uncertainty. They can state a complete fabrication with the same confident tone as a well-known fact, which makes them frustrating to "argue" with and potentially misleading for users who trust them too much. Reducing hallucinations and getting models to express uncertainty are major areas of AI research.
