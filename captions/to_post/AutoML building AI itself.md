🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) is the process of automating the end-to-end tasks of applying machine learning to real-world problems. The goal is to make AI more accessible to non-experts. AutoML tools can automate:
• Data Preprocessing: Cleaning and preparing the data.
• Feature Engineering: Creating new, informative features.
• Model Selection: Choosing the best algorithm for the task (e.g., logistic regression, random forest, neural network).
• Hyperparameter Tuning: Finding the optimal settings for the chosen model.

While AutoML can save a huge amount of time and effort, it's not a magic button. You still need to understand the problem, gather and prepare the data, and interpret the results. As the meme suggests, you have to "build" the AutoML process before it can build your models.
