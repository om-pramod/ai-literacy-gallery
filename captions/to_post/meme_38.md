🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme touches on the importance of user-centric design and summarization in AI applications.

1.  The Curse of Knowledge: An AI has access to vast amounts of data and can perform complex calculations instantly. However, it can suffer from a "curse of knowledge," where it assumes the user wants all the technical details, rather than a simple summary.
2.  Summarization: A key capability of modern LLMs is text summarization. A well-designed AI application should use this capability to distill complex information into a concise and useful answer.
3.  Intent Recognition: The AI should first recognize the user's *intent*. The intent behind "what's the weather like?" is not to receive a scientific report, but to make a decision ("should I bring an umbrella?"). A good AI assistant would recognize this intent and provide a direct, actionable answer (e.g., "Yes, there's a 70% chance of rain this afternoon.").
