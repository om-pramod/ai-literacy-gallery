🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
Voice Cloning, or Voice Synthesis, is a technology that uses AI (typically deep learning models) to create a synthetic, artificial copy of a person's voice.

1.  How it Works: It usually requires a sample of the target person's speech (from a few seconds to a few minutes). A neural network is then trained on this sample to capture the unique characteristics of the voice: pitch, tone, cadence, and accent. Once trained, the model can be used to generate new speech from any text input, in that person's voice.
2.  Ethical Concerns (The "Dual-Use" Problem): This technology has amazing positive uses (e.g., giving a voice to people who have lost theirs). However, it also has significant potential for misuse:
• Scams and Fraud: Impersonating someone for financial gain (e.g., "vishing" - voice phishing).
• Disinformation: Creating fake audio of public figures to spread misinformation.
• Harassment: Creating fake audio to bully or harass someone.
    This is why many researchers and companies in this space are focused on developing detection models (to identify fake audio) and implementing strict ethical guidelines for the use of their technology.
