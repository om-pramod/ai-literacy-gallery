🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a great illustration of the "bias in, bias out" problem in AI.

1.  Training Data Bias: AI models learn from the data they are trained on. If that data contains societal biases (related to race, gender, language, etc.), the model will learn and likely amplify those biases. The internet, being a reflection of humanity, is filled with such biases.
2.  Unwanted Behaviors: A model trained on vast amounts of online conversations might learn that passive-aggressive or sarcastic responses are common, and start to replicate them, even if that wasn't the intended behavior.
3.  Mitigation: Addressing AI bias is a major area of research. Techniques include:
• Data Auditing: Carefully examining and cleaning training data to remove biases.
• Algorithmic Fairness: Designing models and training procedures that are explicitly designed to be fair across different demographic groups.
• Detoxification: Using techniques to filter out toxic or biased language from a model's outputs.
