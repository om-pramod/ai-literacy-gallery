🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme highlights the pros and cons of using Google Colaboratory (Colab) for machine learning.

1.  Pros:
• Free Access to GPUs: Colab provides free access to powerful GPUs (and TPUs), which are essential for training deep learning models. This democratizes AI development.
• Jupyter Notebook Environment: It offers a familiar, easy-to-use Jupyter Notebook interface.
• Zero Configuration: It's a fully managed environment, so you don't have to worry about installing drivers or libraries.
2.  Cons (The source of the stress):
• Resource Limits: The free version has limits on usage. Sessions will time out after a certain period of inactivity or a maximum runtime (e.g., 12 hours). If your training takes longer than that, you'll lose your progress unless you've saved your model checkpoints.
• Non-Persistent Storage: The local file system is ephemeral. When the session ends, your data is gone. You need to save your work to Google Drive or another persistent storage location.
• Variable Resources: You're not guaranteed to get the best GPU, and performance can vary.
