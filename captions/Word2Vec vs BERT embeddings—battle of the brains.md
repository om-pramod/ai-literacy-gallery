🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme touches on the computational intensity of training deep learning models.

The two terms mentioned, Word2Vec and BERT, represent two different eras of word embeddings in Natural Language Processing (NLP).

1.  Word Embeddings: These are numerical representations of words, where words with similar meanings have similar numerical representations. This is how computers can work with text mathematically.
2.  Word2Vec (2013): A classic embedding technique. It learns a single, static vector for each word. It's fast and efficient but has a major limitation: it can't handle polysemy (words with multiple meanings). The embedding for "bank" (river bank) is the same as for "bank" (financial bank).
3.  BERT (2018): A revolutionary, transformer-based model that produces contextualized embeddings. The representation for a word changes depending on the sentence it's in. BERT understands that the "bank" in "I sat by the river bank" is different from the "bank" in "I went to the bank." Training a large model like BERT is incredibly computationally expensive and can take days or even weeks on powerful hardware, hence the "all-night coder" meme.
