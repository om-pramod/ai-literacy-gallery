🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme touches on the psychological and user experience (UX) challenges of autonomous systems.

1.  Human-in-the-Loop: Current autonomous driving systems are mostly Level 2 or Level 3, meaning they require a human to be attentive and ready to take over at any time. This "human-in-the-loop" model can actually increase cognitive load and anxiety, as the human has to constantly monitor the AI.
2.  Trust and Transparency: Building trust in autonomous systems is a major hurdle to their adoption. This requires:
• Reliability: The system must be demonstrably safe and reliable over millions of miles of testing.
• Transparency: The car's interface should clearly communicate what the AI is "seeing" and "thinking." This is why many autonomous cars have a screen showing the other vehicles and obstacles it has detected.
• Predictability: The AI's driving style should be smooth and predictable, not jerky or erratic.
