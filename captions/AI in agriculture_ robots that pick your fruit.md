🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is a funny illustration of the semantic gap in AI.

1.  Semantics: This is the study of meaning. Humans understand that "ripe" in the context of fruit refers to a specific stage of maturity for eating. We have a rich semantic understanding of the world.
2.  Semantic Gap: AI models, especially those trained on limited data, can lack this deep semantic understanding. An AI trained to identify "ripeness" based on visual cues like color and texture might find patterns in an image of an elderly person (e.g., wrinkled skin, certain color tones) that are statistically similar to its training data for ripe fruit. This leads to a classification that is technically "correct" based on its limited data, but semantically and contextually absurd to a human.
