🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme is another great take on the problem of AI hallucinations.

1.  The Source of Hallucinations: LLMs don't "retrieve" facts from a database. They *generate* text, one word at a time, based on the statistical patterns they learned from their training data. A hallucination occurs when the most statistically probable sequence of words does not correspond to reality.
2.  Why it's a Hard Problem:
• Massive, Uncurated Data: The models are trained on a huge portion of the internet, which is full of misinformation, opinions, and fiction. The model can't easily distinguish between fact and fiction.
• Lack of Grounding: The model's "knowledge" is not grounded in the real world. It only knows about the relationships between words.
3.  Mitigation Strategies:
• Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Grounding the model's response in facts retrieved from a reliable knowledge base in real-time.
• Fact-Checking: Using external tools to verify the claims made by the model.
• Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF): Training the model to be more truthful by having humans rate the quality and accuracy of its responses.
