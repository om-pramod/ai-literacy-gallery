🧠 Techie Deep Dive:
This meme touches on the core of how Generative AI for art works.

1.  Training Data: AI art models (like DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion) are trained on massive datasets of images and text descriptions. This includes a significant portion of the art history available on the internet.
2.  Learning Representations: The model learns complex statistical relationships between text prompts and visual elements. When you type "a portrait in the style of Rembrandt," it knows the typical lighting, color palette, and brushwork associated with Rembrandt's work from its training data.
3.  The "Hand" Problem: Interestingly, early AI art models were notoriously bad at generating realistic hands. This was likely because hands are complex, can be in many different positions, and may not have been represented with enough detail and consistency in the training data. This has improved significantly, but it shows that even powerful AI can struggle with specific, complex details.
